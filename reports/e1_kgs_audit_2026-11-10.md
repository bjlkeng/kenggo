# E1 KGS candidate audit

10 November 2026 | Audit `kenggo-6yr.2` | Access investigation `kenggo-6yr.8`

All **1,000 frozen candidate IDs were retrieved and parsed**. Of these, **241 pass the inspected metadata filters** and 759 have at least one exclusion. No record is yet certified as a training-ready human game: historical human status, stable account identity and legal replay remain unverified. This completes a bounded capability audit with deferred capabilities; it does not pass E1 readiness or qualify a model profile.

The [E0 population](../plan/e0_scope_decision.md) remains unchanged: 2025 KGS ordinary ranked human games, both historical ranks 15k–1k or 1d, even 19 x 19 with Black first and no setup stones, Japanese rules with 6.5 komi, 10–30 minutes main time and 3–5 Japanese byo-yomi periods of 20–60 seconds. The sample's metadata-compatible fraction is **24.1% of these candidates**, not an estimate of KGS-wide usable volume.

The [machine-readable manifest](../manifests/e1_kgs_audit_2026-11-10.json) contains aggregate counts, rank pairs, exact clock tuples, property coverage, account-count histograms, page/bundle hashes, budgets and execution history. The [per-game Parquet manifest](../manifests/e1_kgs_audit_games_2026-11-10.parquet) retains all 1,000 source IDs, SGF hashes, metadata, exclusions and audit counters. Original SGFs, archive HTML and request ledgers remain in ignored local run directories. Neither artifact contains the login credentials, cookies, SGF move sequences or comments.

## Access and sampling

The owner supplied an account and explicitly authorized acquisition with conservative load. Authenticated access through the official KGS archive form exposed historical account/month listings and monthly ZIP links. The client used **one connection, at least five seconds between request starts and no automatic retries**. There was no live-room polling, access bypass or external contact.

The earlier [source preflight](e1_source_access_2026-11-10.md) remains the dated record of unauthenticated access. Its policy observations still apply: [robots.txt](https://www.gokgs.com/robots.txt) disallows `/gameArchives.jsp`, and the inspected [service terms](https://www.gokgs.com/tos.jsp) do not establish a blanket research, ML or redistribution licence. The owner's bounded authorization and successful login are not asserted to be KGS-specific automation approval or broader reuse rights. `kenggo-6yr.9` owns the permitted scope of continued collection/reuse and source eligibility before dataset construction or scaling. No raw corpus is distributed in Git.

Discovery used a fixed [public account survey](https://api.github.com/repos/odorizhou/kgs-sgfs-download/git/blobs/6db0c567d37bb78b515aca0d6cd5bbe44f76be3e), generated on 11 April 2026, rather than polling current players. Its 1,632 account labels are discovery hints, not 2025 ranks or human-status evidence. The [original configuration](../manifests/e1_kgs_discovery_2026-11-10.json) selected three handles per exact survey rank by SHA-256 ordering with seed `kenggo-6yr.2-20261110-v1`. The three ordered handles received month sets 1/4/7/10, 2/5/8/11 and 3/6/9/12, giving 48 handles and 192 planned account-months.

The first archive query, for LibertyBot in January 2025, timed out before response headers. Acquisition stopped. A [historical operator statement](https://groups.google.com/g/computer-go-archive/c/PuNvbA19DdM) identifies LibertyBot and GnuGo2 as bots. Before any successful discovery page or SGF, [revision 2](../manifests/e1_kgs_discovery_2026-11-10_v2.json) excluded both handles and their eight slots without replacement. This conservative historical exclusion does not establish current ownership or the cause of the timeout. After a cooldown exceeding five minutes, the remaining 46 handles supplied 184 account-month pages.

The pages contained 16,365 linked rows, 16,327 distinct source URLs and **7,848 candidate IDs** passing the archive filters: ranked, 19 x 19 without handicap, both ranks in range and a 2025 date. Nine inaccessible rows had no SGF URL—seven Free and two Review—and were counted separately without invented IDs. Historical archive ranks, followed by SGF cross-checks, determine scope. The HTML's board-size label alone does not establish the required komi.

Before any SGF retrieval, URL IDs were deduplicated and sorted within weaker-rank/month strata by SHA-256 of the seed and URL. Round-robin selection across month 1–12 and rank 15k–1d redistributed empty strata within the frozen frame. It selected exactly 1,000 IDs in 103 monthly bundles. No retrieval failure or SGF exclusion caused resampling. Month counts from January to December are **77, 71, 110, 83, 63, 105, 70, 71, 96, 62, 92 and 100**.

This is a convenience frame with 2026 survivor, activity, room/language, rank and account-month biases. It cannot estimate the full 2025 population or split-adjusted usable volume. All original discovery account-month slots, the selected IDs and the preliminary `cu2` September 2025 probe must stay out of reserved final evaluation when E1 constructs splits.

## Exclusions and provenance

Every selected SGF parsed with pinned SGFmill 1.1.1. Parsing verifies syntax and inspected properties, not captures, ko, scoring or a correct played line. The audit follows the leftmost variation and excludes branching games until their played line can be established.

Reasons overlap; their sum is not the number of excluded games. The distinct excluded count is 759. No exact byte duplicates were found. Three excess matching move-sequence fingerprints occur among the zero-move records; they are possible duplicates for later inspection, not proof that the source IDs identify the same game.

All 1,000 records have both player handles, historical ranks, dates, rules, komi, board size and main-time metadata. `OT` is absent in 63 records and `RE` in 14. No event or round property was observed. Absence of those fields does not verify ordinary non-tournament/non-simultaneous play. The archive filter rejects Free/Review/Teaching types; **971 selected records still have unverified human status**, separate from the 29 historical bot-handle records. The source schema itself warns that player flags in game summaries only reliably describe rank. ([KGS data types](https://www.gokgs.com/json/dataTypes.html))

All selected URL dates and SGF dates agree. Archive timestamps are labeled UTC; the SGF date has day precision. The per-game source URL, exact ZIP date/filename, SGF hash and page/bundle provenance allow the retained originals to be traced. The 103 bundles contain 12,288 SGF members. Only the selected 1,000 were parsed; additional members remain unaudited.

## Timing and capability decisions

All 224,274 observed move nodes have finite nonnegative mover post-clock values; the compatible subset has all 52,900. Missing and invalid post-clock counts are zero in this sample. This does not establish valid elapsed-time labels. [SGF `BL`/`WL`](https://www.red-bean.com/sgf/properties.html) report time after a move, and overtime/reset behavior still needs a KGS-specific interpretation.

| Timing observation | All records | Compatible subset |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Actions with `OB`/`OW` overtime markers | 10,810 | 2,321 |
| Observed clock increases | 258 | 67 |
| Main-time difference candidates | 195,998 | 50,330 |
| Other observed clock actions requiring interpretation | 28,276 | 2,570 |
| Validated elapsed-time labels | **0** | **0** |

A main-time difference candidate requires consecutive usable clocks for the same color, a nonincreasing value above the stated Japanese period and no current overtime marker. Missing/invalid clocks and overtime markers break the chain. These provisional counts do not authorize duration targets; ambiguous time stays masked, never zero. Target-move time, post-move clocks and results remain labels only, not pre-move inputs. The compatible subset contains 14 exact nominal clock tuples, retained in the manifest.

Keep the original population and defer promotion of every proposed raw profile (15k, 10k, 5k, 1k and 1d). The 5k evidence is particularly sparse after filtering: four handles, five player-game appearances, 448 actions and one pass. No rank is qualified by move counts alone, and no post-split volume estimate is defensible from this frame. Do not expand dates, relax komi or add an endgame heuristic to manufacture support.

Personalization is deferred because stable identities and sufficient chronological histories are unverified. Duration modeling and timing-based adaptive search are deferred until elapsed labels are validated. Core rank conditioning remains conditional on human/ordinary-game eligibility, account grouping, legal replay, canonical whole-game splits and masked inputs. `kenggo-6yr.9` blocks construction/readiness for the newly identified eligibility and identity issues; the existing `.3`–`.7` gates remain open. There is no training run, strength calibration, qualified raw opponent pool or B weight transfer in this audit.

## Costs and reproduction

The recorded acquisition ceiling was 500 requests, 100 MiB of response bodies, 2 MiB per HTML response, 8 MiB per ZIP, 256 MiB declared expanded ZIP content, 60 wall minutes, 15 CPU minutes and zero GPU work. Checks run before each request; a response is bounded by its byte limit and a 30-second socket timeout. The unchanged UTC deadline was **19:58:13**. It was not reset by continuation.

The four acquisition processes consumed **297 requests and 33,730,402 captured response bytes**: 296 HTTP-200 responses and one timeout before headers. Instrumented collector wall time totaled **1,930.746 seconds** and process CPU **6.524 seconds** (0.001812 CPU-hours). The first-request to last-response window, including cooldown and diagnosis gaps, was **18:58:28.573–19:41:15.446 UTC**, or 2,566.873 seconds. The measured minimum interval within an attempt was 5.00008 seconds; attempts were farther apart. There were no further network errors, throttle responses or automatic retries.

After the timeout, two successful page requests exposed local parser failures. Two additional logins beyond revision 2's planned two were recorded before continuation. The first successful page had not yet been cached and was repeated once; the next response was cached and reused after the `colspan` repair. Every attempt remains charged to the original budgets. Capture-time code hashes are retained in the manifest; later offline date/cache/inaccessible-row checks preserved the frozen selection.

The earlier authenticated route probe is separate: **four HTTP-200 requests, 11,474 bytes and no SGFs**. Combined authenticated work therefore used 301 KGS requests and 33,741,876 captured body bytes. The earlier unauthenticated preflight's 12 checks and 110,965 bytes are separately recorded. Public GitHub discovery and web search are outside those KGS totals. Bytes measure captured bodies, not network/TLS overhead or server compute; the timed-out request's server work is unknown.

Downloaded ZIPs declare 57,683,710 expanded bytes; the selected SGFs total 4,645,655 bytes. Offline inspection and per-game artifact writing took **4.615 wall seconds and 4.838 CPU seconds** (0.001344 CPU-hours), under its 300-wall-second/120-CPU-second, 64-MiB-selected-byte and 1-MiB-per-SGF limits. It used no GPU. Audit timing excludes interpreter startup and final summary writing. Acquisition CPU includes process startup but excludes final ledger writing; its instrumented wall time excludes gaps between processes. Dependency setup, tests, authoring, independent review and owner effort were not comprehensively timed. These are resource observations, not throughput comparisons or projected training costs.
