retains both the deliberately partial attempt and its resumed attempt. After
eight updates, the check saved a checkpoint and executed one control update.
Restoring the checkpoint reproduced that update's metrics and every model
tensor exactly, including the next batch and clock-dropout RNG state.

Training continued to step 16. The final checkpoint was uploaded, downloaded
and verified against its pinned manifest; model tensors and the next-step
cursor matched. The artifact is
`bjlkeng/kenggo/8b5c91d3fca64e7b9d9880fe77a23b98-step-16-5e4b074c:v0`.

All 17 executed updates and 136 row presentations count toward the check.
They used 1.683244240 calling-process CPU seconds, 11.862086306 elapsed seconds,
653,373 attempted checkpoint-upload bytes and zero GPU allocation, within the
180 CPU-second/300 elapsed-second/10 MiB allowance. W&B service CPU is outside
the calling-process measurement. The check accessed no development,
confirmation or final target rows. The two excluded source games remain
excluded by the pinned v3 inputs.

## Behavior and environment checks

Fifteen focused learner tests cover the default architecture, learned unknown
ranks, rank-free ablation, invalid-input masking, legal human-action loss,
recorded-result signs, timeout/forfeit exclusion, clock dropout, deterministic
epochs and exact checkpoint continuation across an epoch boundary. A small
synthetic training problem learns distinct moves and opposite mover values;
the check is stronger than a successful forward pass.

The combined suite passes **276 tests with zero skips** in both the pinned CPU
environment and the CUDA environment running CPU tests. Both runs enable the
pinned native rules comparison. Lock/sync checks, CPU smoke, package build,
whitespace and changed Markdown links pass. No additional GPU test was needed;
the separately recorded GPU-readiness check remains the CUDA evidence.

Held-out comparisons, external-reference integration and complete learned-policy
games remain separate E2 acceptance work. The implementation check does not
measure throughput, select a checkpoint by validation score or consume the
E0 GPU probe/SL/RL allowances. Independent review and delivery evidence are
recorded in the owning Bead.
