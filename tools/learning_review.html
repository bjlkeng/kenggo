Learning curves and previous runs

Policy loss (NLL) measures error on the recorded human move. Value loss (MSE) measures error predicting the eventual winner. Lower is better for both; move agreement is better when higher.

Training and validation use different data and averaging; their gap alone does not measure overfitting. Value losses exclude masked outcomes.
