Four models, one training seed. Development move prediction and raw-game behavior are separate results. No opponent is promoted by this page.

Move prediction

NLL measures how much probability the model gave the recorded human move; lower is better. A value of +∞ means it gave a recorded move zero probability; that is a measured failure, not missing data. Move agreement counts when its top choice matches. Outcome MSE measures error against valid recorded game results; lower is better.

Paired differences on the same positions

First model minus second model. A negative NLL difference favors the first. The useful reduction is 0.02 nats per move; ordinary 95% intervals are descriptive and do not establish promotion.
